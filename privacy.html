Privacy Policy — Nepali Patro (BS First Calendar)

Last updated: 21 Sep 2026

Nepali Patro is a static web app with no backend server. All data is stored locally in your browser.

Data we access

  • With your consent, we access your Google Calendar and Google Tasks via googleapis.com to list, create, update and delete events/tasks you choose to sync. Scopes: calendar, tasks, userinfo.email/profile.
  • We access your basic profile (name, email, avatar) to show who is signed in.

Storage

  • OAuth tokens and user info are stored in localStorage in your browser (np_g_token, np_g_user) and never sent to any server other than Google.
  • Calendar events you create locally are stored in localStorage as well.
  • Clearing site data or signing out revokes and removes tokens.

Sharing

We do not share, sell, or transmit your data to any third party except Google’s APIs you authorize. Each visitor’s data is isolated to their browser.

Contact

For questions, open an issue in the repository or contact the site owner listed in the OAuth consent screen.
